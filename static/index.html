2. What the Reconstruction (x̂) Means

ceVAE+ was trained strictly on healthy anatomy with zero skip connections. It attempts to 'heal' the scan by reconstructing what healthy parenchyma would look like in this location.

Key Benefit: Zero pathology leakage (lesions are erased, not copied)

3. What the Subtraction (|x - x̂|) Means

Subtracting the reconstruction from your input reveals what the healthy model could not explain. Normal structures cancel to near zero (dark), leaving abnormal lesions glowing bright.
